# Reporting vulnerabilities in that plugin
If you detect any **bugs or vulnerabilities** in the plugin, you can send an issue to me in order that I can fix it as soon as I can.  
Unfortunately, **sometimes I cannot solve your problem successfully**. However, your issues will be read and opened until the bugs are gone.
